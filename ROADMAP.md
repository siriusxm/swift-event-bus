# Roadmap

This document describes how `EventBus` is maintained and released. It sets expectations rather than making binding commitments: the cadences below are minimums ("no less than") and work may happen more frequently.

## Intake review

Community input — bug reports, feature requests, and questions — is reviewed no less than monthly. We triage incoming issues, respond, and fold feedback into planning.

## Releases

Tagged releases are published no less than quarterly, gathering the changes accumulated since the previous release.

## Hotfixes

Blocking bugs — issues that prevent correct use of the library or introduce regressions in supported functionality — are addressed out of band with a hotfix release, without waiting for the next scheduled release.

## Direction

The project's direction is steered internally by SiriusXM (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)). This roadmap describes process and cadence, not a feature commitment, and is subject to change.
