# Roadmap

This document describes how `EventBus` is maintained and released. It sets guidance for the project's intake and release cadence.

## Intake review

Community input — bug reports, feature requests, and questions — are continually reviewed. They are formally triaged on a monthly cadence.

## Releases

Tagged releases are published on a quarterly cadence, gathering the changes accumulated since the previous release.

## Hotfixes

Bugs may be addressed more quickly with out-of-band releases at the team's discretion.

## Direction

The project's direction is steered internally by SiriusXM (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)).
